Privacy Policy

Emoji Rain: Focus or Fail  ·  by ChAs Tech Group

01 Overview

Emoji Rain does not collect, store, or sell your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how ChAs Tech Group ("we", "our", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game Emoji Rain: Focus or Fail ("the App"), available on Google Play.

By downloading or playing Emoji Rain, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

02 Information We Collect

We do not directly collect any personal data from users.

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your high score and game progress
  • Sound preferences (on/off)
  • Remove Ads purchase status and expiry date

None of this data is transmitted to our servers. It lives entirely on your device and is cleared if you uninstall the App.

03 Advertising (Google AdMob)

Emoji Rain is free to play and is supported by advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to show you relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (resettable in your device settings)
  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • Approximate location (country/region level)
  • App usage and interaction data
  • IP address

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by ChAs Tech Group. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation
  • Or reset your Advertising ID in device settings

Purchasing Remove Ads removes all banner and interstitial ads. Rewarded ads (used for gameplay features like continuing after game over) remain available as optional, user-initiated interactions.

04 In-App Purchases

Emoji Rain offers optional time-limited Remove Ads purchases ($0.99 / $2.99 / $8.99). All payment processing is handled exclusively by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment card details.

Purchase records are managed by Google Play. To request a refund, contact Google Play Support directly.

06 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

⚠️ Emoji Rain is rated for users aged 13 and above due to advertising content.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Parental guidance is recommended for users under 16, in accordance with applicable regulations.

07 Data Security & Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data stored on our servers to secure. All game data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

Local game data (scores, preferences) is retained until you uninstall the App or clear the App's data in your device settings.

08 Your Rights (GDPR & Global)

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

For ad-related data rights, exercise these directly through Google's Data & Privacy dashboard. For any other requests, contact us below.
